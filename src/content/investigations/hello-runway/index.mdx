Every investigation on this site starts the same way: a documented data pipeline,
a handful of interactive charts, and prose that explains what you're looking at.
This post is the pre-flight check — it exercises the chart wrapper, the dark
theme, and the shared state store before "How States Spend, Save, and Borrow"
takes off.

## A chart, for demonstration purposes

Nothing here is a real dataset — just a week of made-up flight departures, rendered
through the same `EChart` component and `tarmac` ECharts theme every future chart
will use.
